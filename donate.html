A Word About Finances

We see ourselves as a bridge between resources and needs. We know people. And we want to use that knowledge to get the right resources into the right people’s hands at the right time.

Overhead

We think we are a good use of your money. Other than a (legally required) small insurance payment, we have no overhead. We pay no mortgage. We pay no building costs. We don’t pay for vehicles. Our goal is to leverage the resources of our community in the most efficient and effective way possible.
